Layout automation, V2

Vijay Gupta and Navin Kumar Mishra · SkyCadEda

A month ago we showed our layout automation, and it had many quality issues. Since then we have made many improvements:

  • Optional half-cell layouts, where the circuit allows
  • Guard rings and shields
  • Matched layout for differential signals
  • Optimized routing, with two vias everywhere
  • Decap filler
  • EM-aware layouts that change with the current

We rewrote our layout engine, GOAL (Generator Of Analog Layout), from scratch in Rust, which made it 10× faster: every layout now takes tens to hundreds of milliseconds. The demo also shows EM/IR results. Every feature is configurable, including the aspect ratio.
